The rapier was a civilian sword that emerged in Europe during the Renaissance. Distinguished by its long, narrow blade and complex hand guard, it was optimized for precise thrusting attacks rather than powerful cuts.

Widely carried by nobles and gentlemen, the rapier became the preferred weapon for duels and personal defense. Its lightweight design and emphasis on speed contributed to the development of sophisticated fencing systems.

Though less effective on battlefields dominated by armor, the rapier played a major role in the history of European martial arts and remains one of the most iconic fencing weapons ever created.
