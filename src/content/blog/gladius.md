The gladius was the primary sword of Roman legionaries during the height of the Roman Empire. Featuring a short double-edged blade, it was optimized for thrusting attacks in tight battlefield formations.

Roman soldiers used the gladius alongside large shields, allowing them to strike effectively while remaining protected. Its design proved highly effective in disciplined infantry combat.

The success of the gladius contributed significantly to Rome's military achievements, and it remains one of the most famous swords in history.
