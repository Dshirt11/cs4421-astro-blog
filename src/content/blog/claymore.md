The claymore is a traditional Scottish greatsword associated with Highland warriors. Featuring a long double-edged blade and distinctive downward-sloping crossguard, it was designed for powerful two-handed use.

The weapon gained prominence during the 15th and 16th centuries, where it was valued for its reach and ability to deliver devastating strikes in combat. Its size made it particularly effective against multiple opponents.

Today, the claymore remains one of Scotland's most recognizable historical weapons and a lasting symbol of Highland courage and heritage.
