The falchion was a European sword characterized by its single-edged blade and forward-weighted design. Its shape often resembled a large knife or machete, making it ideal for delivering powerful cutting attacks.

Used throughout the Middle Ages, the falchion was favored by both infantry and mounted warriors. It offered excellent striking power while remaining lighter and more maneuverable than many polearms.

Although fewer examples survive compared to other medieval swords, the falchion remains notable for its unique design and effectiveness in combat.
