The sabre emerged as a highly effective weapon for mounted combat, featuring a curved blade that allowed riders to strike opponents while moving at speed. Its design balanced cutting power with agility.

Used extensively by cavalry across Europe, Asia, and the Americas, the sabre remained a military weapon for centuries. Different nations developed their own variations to suit specific combat styles.

Even after its battlefield role declined, the sabre continued to be used in military ceremonies and remains one of the three weapons employed in modern fencing.
